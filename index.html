Standard Clinical Criteria
Scoliosis
Spine X-Ray

Lateral vertebral column curvature with Cobb angle exceeding 10 degrees.

Spondylolisthesis
Spine X-Ray

Translational displacement (anterior or posterior slipping) of one vertebra over another.

Herniated Disc
Lumbar MRI

Protrusion of intervertebral disc material impinging adjacent lumbar nerve roots.

Thecal Sac Stenosis
Lumbar MRI

Narrowing of the central spinal canal with circumferential compression of the thecal sac.
